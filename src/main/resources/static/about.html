About ELearn

Empowering education through technology — anytime, anywhere.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a modern, accessible, and interactive learning environment that connects students with passionate teachers, enabling knowledge sharing without boundaries.

🌍

Our Vision

A world where quality education is available to everyone, regardless of location or background. We believe in the power of digital learning to transform lives and communities.

💡

Our Approach

We combine structured course content with real-time interaction, quizzes, and progress tracking to create an engaging and effective learning experience.

Platform Features

Everything you need for a complete e-learning experience

📚

Course Management

Create, edit, and organize courses with multiple lessons and resources.

📝

Interactive Quizzes

Test knowledge with MCQ quizzes attached to each lesson.

💬

Real-Time Chat

WebSocket-powered live chat rooms for each course.

📊

Progress Tracking

Track your learning journey with visual progress bars.

📎

File Sharing

Upload and download course materials — PDFs, slides, and more.

📢

Announcements

Teachers can post important updates for enrolled students.
